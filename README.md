# AI-Powered Customer Inquiry Chatbot

## Overview
This project involved designing an AI-powered chatbot to handle repetitive customer inquiries for a Spa & Fitness Center. The goal was to improve response time, reduce staff workload, and enhance customer experience.

---

## Business Problem
A Spa & Fitness Center received high volumes of repetitive inquiries through their website, including:

- Pricing requests
- Service availability
- Booking questions
- Operating hours

Manual handling led to:
- Delayed responses
- Reduced customer satisfaction
- Staff overload
- Missed inquiry opportunities 

---

## Objective
Design a conversational automation solution to handle common customer inquiries while maintaining service quality and structured tracking.

---

## Solution Approach
- Analyzed inquiry patterns to identify automation opportunities  
- Designed conversational flows based on user intent  
- Built structured response pathways for high-frequency queries  
- Configured escalation triggers for complex inquiries  
- Integrated with human support when necessary  
- Logged interactions in Dataverse for reporting  

---

## Tools & Technologies
- Power Virtual Agents  
- Power Automate  
- Dataverse  
- Microsoft 365  

---

## Results
- Automated approximately 80% of recurring inquiries  
- Significantly improved customer response time  
- Reduced staff workload  
- Standardized response consistency  
- Enabled structured tracking of inquiry trends
